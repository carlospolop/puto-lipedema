Intervention(s)
Group 1: Group 1 is characterized by patients with lipedema stage 2 or 3 who have had a conventionally performed tumescence liposuction between January 2018 and January 2024.
Pre- and postoperative we took bloods to check via laboratory diagnostics.
Group 2: Group 2 includes patients with lipedema stage 2 or 3 wo had a waterjet assited liposuction between January 2021 and 2024

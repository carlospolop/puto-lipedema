2. WHO ICTRP Database
The World Health Organization, through its International Clinical Trials Registry Platform, has developed the ICTRP database to provide patients, family members and members of the public current information about clinical research studies. The ICTRP database contains information about clinical trials being conducted throughout the world. This data is provided to the ICTRP by registries conforming to WHO standards. ICTRP is updated weekly. Trial data posted on this search portal are not endorsed by WHO, but are provided as a service to our users.

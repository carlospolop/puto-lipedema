DRKS00034711

Waterjet-assisted Liposuction reduces inflammation but increases the risk of hypokalemia when compared to conventional tumescent liposuction in patients with lipedema

Brief summary in scientific language

A total of 47 patients with lipedema (stage 2 or 3) were treated with the conventional tumescent liposuction and 25 patients with Waterjet-assisted liposuction (WAL). Pre- and postoperative laboratory diagnostics had been done.

Health condition or problem studied

ICD-10-GM (translation):
E88.21
Healthy volunteers:
No

Interventions, Observational Groups

Arm 1:
Group 1 is characterized by patients with lipedema stage 2 or 3 who have had a conventionally performed tumescence liposuction between January 2018 and January 2024. Pre- and postoperative we took bloods to check via laboratory diagnostics.
Arm 2:
Group 2 includes patients with lipedema stage 2 or 3 wo had a waterjet assited liposuction between January 2021 and 2024

Endpoints

Primary outcome:
January 2024 patients who had liposuction between january 2018 and January 2024 are included pre- and postoperative the following parameters were measured: hematocrit hemoglobine leucocytes thrombocytes sodium potassium
Secondary outcome:
15th July 2024 till 15th July 2024 relevant data of patients treated between January 2018 - January 2024 was collected

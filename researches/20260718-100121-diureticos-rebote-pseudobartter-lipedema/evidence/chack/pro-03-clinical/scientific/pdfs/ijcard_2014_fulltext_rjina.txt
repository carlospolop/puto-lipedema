Title: Lowering furosemide dose in stable chronic heart failure patients with reduced ejection fraction is not accompanied by decompensation: A randomized study

URL Source: http://www.internationaljournalofcardiology.com/article/S0167-5273(14)01876-2/fulltext

Markdown Content:
Diuretics are the cornerstone of heart failure (HF) therapy. Over 80% of ambulatory patients suffering from HF are treated with a diuretic [[1]](http://www.internationaljournalofcardiology.com/article/S0167-5273(14)01876-2/fulltext#)
